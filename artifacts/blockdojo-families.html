For ninjas and families

The cave on the TV is your dojo’s coin book. You earn coins in class. You earn EXP for the same work. You spend coins at the shop and at the fridge.

THE CAVE

What the game is

Block Dojo is the cave you see at the dojo. It is not a second class. It is how the center keeps your coins, your EXP, and your belt in one place.

On the TV

  • The mine is the cave’s coin pile. Pays come out of it.
  • When a sensei pays you, your name runs across the screen.
  • Belt up plays that belt’s show on the whole TV.
  • The EXP button opens a board of every ninja, A to Z, with a bar toward the biggest EXP prize.

On your card

  • One ninja, one card. A second card does not start a new pile of coins.
  • Your sensei searches your name and pays that card.
  • You do not tap the shop yourself. You ask, and the sensei redeems or claims it.
  • Parents: this is dojo coins, not a charge to your card at home.
Block Dojo · families2

COINS

How you earn them

Coins are for spending. They come off your card when you buy something.

A payout

Up to 25

A sensei can give 5, 10, or 25 at a time. They wait 15 seconds before paying you again.

A day

75 on your card

That is the cap for class payouts, including welcome coins when you first join. It does not reset if someone types your name again.

Bonuses

Outside the 75

Daily and weekly achievements add extra coins. They do not count against the cap. You can earn each one once in that period.

Where coins go

  • Shop prizes that say “coins.”
  • Anything from the fridge. That part is new. The next page is the rule.
  • If a sensei undoes a payout, those coins leave your card.

They are not cash

You cannot buy coins. You earn them by coding, helping, and the challenges your sensei posts. If the card is short, you earn more next class. Nobody charges a parent at the fridge.

Welcome coins: 10, and they count today Same EXP as the coins you earn

EXP

It stays with you

EXP is the long path. Coins get spent. EXP does not.

How EXP goes up

  • Every coin you earn adds the same EXP. A +10 payout is +10 EXP.
  • Welcome coins and daily or weekly achievements count too.
  • A sensei fixing a balance does not add EXP.
  • If a payout or achievement is undone, that EXP comes back off.

How you use it

  • You do not spend it. A claim does not lower the number.
  • EXP prizes can be claimed once, when your EXP is high enough and your belt is high enough.
  • On the TV, open EXP. Find your name. The bar shows the way to the top EXP prize.
  • The list is alphabetical. It is not a leaderboard.

THE SHOP

What you can claim

Ask a sensei. They pick your name, then tap the prize. You need the price and the belt. Coin prizes also need to be in stock.

Sticker Pack

15 coins · any belt

Sensei taps Redeem. 15 coins leave your card. One pack leaves the shelf.

Code Patch

25 coins · Yellow+

Same as a coin redeem. You need a Yellow belt or higher.

LED Keychain

40 coins · any belt

Coins come off your card. The sensei hands you the keychain.

3D Printed Dragon

80 coins · Green+

Green belt or higher. Coins leave your card. One dragon leaves the shelf.

Mini Figure

120 coins · Blue+

Blue belt or higher. A coin redeem, same as the smaller prizes.

Treasure Chest

200 EXP · Purple+ · once

This one is EXP, not coins. Your EXP stays. You can claim it one time.

Coin prizes can be bought again while they are in stock. The fridge is not in this shop. Fridge items are paid in coins at the desk, then taken.

BELT 1 OF 13 · CODE NINJAS CREATE

White

Introduction to IMPACT and coding with blocks

What your ninja learns

  • Make a sprite, the character in your game.
  • Put instructions in order so the game does one thing, then the next.
  • Move the sprite and notice when it overlaps something else.
  • Use variables, functions, and the loop that keeps a game running.
  • Fire projectiles, use a simple “if,” and add animation and music.

This is the start. There is no white-belt show. Your first ceremony on the TV is Yellow.

Advanced coding with blocks
